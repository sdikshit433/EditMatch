How EditMatch Works

For Editors

  1. Create your profile with your details, pricing, and availability.
  2. Upload up to 5 portfolio videos to showcase your style.
  3. TinyFish AI analyzes your editing style, pacing, and visual effects to build your smart skill profile.
  4. Receive high-intent client invitations tailored specifically to your strengths.

For Clients

  1. Fill out a quick form describing your project, budget, and desired editing style.
  2. Our TinyFish AI Agent processes your requirements and extracts key editing preferences.
  3. Get matched with the top editors on our platform whose proven style perfectly fits your vision.
  4. Review your AI-matched editors and send instant project invitations.
